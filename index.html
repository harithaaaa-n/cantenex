Campus Canteen Terms

1. Zero-Queue Verification: All pre-orders must be claimed within 15 minutes of the selected break slot using the digital QR token.

2. Freshness Guarantee: Items are prepared hot according to your scheduled slot. Uncollected orders after 30 minutes may be cleared to maintain kitchen hygiene.

3. Modifications: You may cancel or amend an order before the kitchen status transitions to 'PREPARING'.

Student Data & Privacy

1. Student Information: Your Name, Register Number, and Department are collected solely for counter dispatch and identity verification.

2. Local Persistence: All demo transaction tokens and tray selections are securely stored in your local campus browser session.
